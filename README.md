## What it is

Flow is a calm budget planner for busy brains. It keeps one number in view — what is safe to spend until payday — and makes logging a purchase a three-tap job.

The demo is the real planner with sample data in it. Bills, envelopes, goals, the month view, the day plan and the ADHD tax check all work. You can log up to 20 entries of your own.

## Your data stays on your device

The page makes no network requests: no server, no account, no tracking. Everything you type is saved in your own browser.

## If you decide to buy

When the demo is full it offers to download your entries as a file. The full planner opens that file exactly as you left it, so nothing has to be typed twice.
